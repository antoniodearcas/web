92. "I am here to serve you"

...to which the ego responds: "Get away from me!"

In this letter, I explore the purpose of the Universe and everything within it. If the Universe is one, its purpose must be one as well. What is that unified intention? What constant message does the Universe whisper to you through every experience, every encounter, and every moment of your life?

92. “I am here to serve you”

This morning I had an intuition I want to share. The purpose of the Universe is to serve you, like a divine physician accompanying you on your path of healing. Healing is not of the body. The body is just another part of the Universe, and therefore shares its purpose of serving you. True healing is of your mind, which transcends the Universe but has forgotten its divine origin and fallen into a dream of separation and death.

Everything in the Universe is here to serve you on that journey back home. The trees accompany you, sustaining your body with their oxygen and opening your heart with their beauty. The table at your workplace, the tissues in your pocket, the traffic light on the street… everything is here for you.

Even the neighbor downstairs who starts playing the drums at two in the morning, the dead pigeon on the sidewalk, the package that doesn’t arrive, the traffic ticket, the conversation that triggers your emotions… all of it is here to serve you.

Because within you there are hidden things that, under the radar of your consciousness, cloud your vision and weigh on your heart. Judgments against others, or, what is the same, against yourself. Traumas and blocked emotions that, after spending so much time with you, have become identities: I am a shy person, irritable, anxious, melancholic…

You are none of that, and the entire Universe is here to remind you.

Those things you carry inside, and hide with your defense mechanisms, are the cause of all the suffering you will experience in this world. Sooner or later, you will have to let them go. There is no other option: everything built from fear will eventually dissolve. The only question is when.

All of us will sooner or later go through this process of purging. But we are not alone. The entire Universe is here to serve us.

If you were on a battlefield and received a gunshot, would you resist the physician who wants to open the wound to remove the bullet?

Jesus said: “If you bring forth what is within you, what you bring forth will save you.
If you do not bring forth what is within you, what you do not bring forth will destroy you.” (Gospel of Thomas, Logion 70)

The Physician operates through the entire Universe, but can only remove the bullet and heal the wound if we cooperate with Him. To cooperate with Him is to stop denying the wound (our judgments, our beliefs based in separation, and our emotions rooted in fear) look at it with courage, and freely choose: I let this holy instant wash my mind of fear, hate, or guilt. I let the Physician reorder my mind and cleanse my eyes so I can see the truth again: that God is only Love, and the Universe is here to help me remember it.

Only a few humans have allowed the Physician to fully heal their minds. Buddha, Jesus, Krishna, and all the sages, saints, and divine teachers who have passed through this world did the same: they abandoned their personal purposes, denied their separate individuality, and let the universal purpose act through their bodies. Their lives then became a perfect expression of this essential principle: I am here to serve you, to show you where you are judging, and to help you see again.

Everything you perceive, every object, person, or event in your life, shares this holy purpose.

But to recognize it, you must allow yourself to be reoriented toward that purpose. If you do not know you are walking toward Santiago de Compostela, the signs along the path mean nothing to you. Likewise, if you do not know that your destiny is to heal your mind from the illusion of separation and to recognize yourself again in the unity of Love, you will not perceive how the entire Universe is helping you achieve exactly that.

If your purpose is to cling to the illusion of separation, the world will seem chaotic, as though the Universe itself were trying to strip you of what little you hold. In this state, the darkness around you will feel like proof that your judgments are justified.

Living this way is like living as Adam in Michelangelo’s fresco: God, through the entire Universe, trying to reach you and touch you with His grace; you raise your arm to ask for help, but not really, and your hand drops, in no hurry to leave the dream of pleasure and pain. You will be like Saint Augustine when he prayed: “Lord, grant me chastity and continence… but not yet.” (Confessions, Book VIII, chapter 7)

William Blake - Cain Fleeing from the Wrath of God (The Body of Abel Found by Adam and Eve)

If, on the other hand, you remember that your purpose is Love, you will begin to see the darkness as a projection of your mind and turn inward rather than outward, seeking the source of that darkness within yourself. You will realize that what pains you most is nothing more than a finger pointing to the wound you are meant to heal. It is helping you. What once seemed to be your enemy is now revealed as Christ in disguise, coming to you again and again, despite the darkness you hurled at Him. What once caused you suffering will become the very path through which God reminds you of the Love in which your existence is held.

Recognizing this is to live with gratitude for the perfection of the cosmos. Everything is in its place, and all suffering will cease when you are ready to take the leap to a broader horizon. Until then, you can rest in this knowledge, knowing you are in good company, being led by the hand, inevitably, toward the dissolution of all veils.

With all my love,

A.
