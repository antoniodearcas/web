111. Life beyond the clouds

On cultivating inner freedom

There is nothing in this world that the Spirit cannot transform into a path of liberation, into a path toward God.

I stepped onto the entrepreneurial path believing it would be about business, structure, strategy, sales, money and marketing.

Four months in, I'm beginning to realize it isn't. That's only the surface, and it must be studied and taken care of, of course. But at its core, this is the same as everything else. This, too, is about God, and my only function is to forgive.

This morning, in a conversation with the community, someone asked me: do you feel fear or anxiety about launching this new project? The answer is yes, but only in part. Thoughts of fear, insecurity, and doubt pass through my mind… but they are like clouds, and they say nothing about who I am. My work is to remain aware of the sky behind them, which is what I truly am: an open space of infinite potential. There, there is only total certainty. The certainty that this does not arise from me as a separate individual, but from an intelligence and a power far greater.

From there, my role is not to push this forward, but to allow it to be. The way to do that is to observe those thoughts with compassion and, seeing through them as the illusion they are, ask the Holy Spirit to dissolve them. That is all I have to do, and everything else unfolds on its own.

Throughout our lives, we have built an identity based on our history, on what we believe we are, on what others believe we are, on all the patterns of thought and emotion we have repeated again and again. All of that generates an energetic pattern, the ego, which is what we mistakenly call "I."

And what happens is that our consciousness, which is infinite, finds itself apparently trapped inside that character. Any sign of growth or change is interpreted as a threat, and so it tries to avoid all forms of novelty or adventure. We have built a cage for our soul, which, like everything that lives, wants to grow, expand, and fully enjoy the gift of life. Hence the phrase that always comes back to me from Dylan: "He not busy being born is busy dying."

Spiritual practice opens that inner space: it allows us to recognize our true vastness and the freedom that is our divine inheritance. As we cultivate that awareness, life begins to be lived differently, not from fear or scarcity, but from truth, where nothing is lacking. We stop building on sand and begin to build on rock.

This is why spiritual practice integrates so naturally with entrepreneurship and with any creative process. When beginning something new, that limited structure fires up and says: "I'm not capable, I'm not ready, who am I to do this?" But instead of stopping there, we ask for Help so that those apparent limitations may be dissolved, and we keep moving forward, with clarity, passing through the clouds, propelled by a strength and a joy that no thought or emotion can stop.

That capacity to transcend one's own mind is what is allowing me to move forward, to step out of who I thought I was, and begin embodying a wider identity in the world.

This is what I practice every morning before showing up to the daily meditation: I advance with my machete of light through the thicket of the limited self, which fights to maintain its smallness.

By the time I sit down in front of the screen at eight o'clock, I feel serene, expansive, fully surrendered. And then, from that stillness, I begin to guide a meditation where I teach the very capacity I have just practiced. Very meta!

With all my love,

Antonio
