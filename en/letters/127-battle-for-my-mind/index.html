127. Battle for My Mind

Is there a demon in my aura?... Yes.

Encouraged by the members of my community, Between Heaven and Earth, I am finally sharing a letter I wrote during my last semester at Berklee, and never dared to send.

Yesterday, around 3 in the afternoon, I decided to do a qigong practice that consists of meditating standing up, holding different postures for several minutes each. That is what the body does, but the real work is in the mind. It begins with the visualization of a sphere of light a few centimeters above the head. It is a large sphere, about a meter in diameter, and it represents wuji, the Chinese name for "All that is." This sphere contains everything, all that was, all that is and will be; all the answers, all intelligence, all love and power. It is a hologram of totality, and it shares all the qualities of the divine. For the first 15 minutes, one concentrates on this sphere.

When the bell sounds, attention moves to the center of the Earth, where one visualizes a large golden sphere. This is the energy or essence of Mother Nature. For 15 minutes, one connects with this center and, with each inhalation, pumps this golden energy upward through the legs, storing it in the abdomen.

The third part consists of fusing these two spheres in the abdomen, so for 15 minutes, the inhalation draws both spheres toward the navel, and on the exhale they return each to their position, above and below. This practice is designed for one to merge with Heaven and Earth, becoming a bridge between the two. In Christian terms, it would be an energetic training in "Thy Will be done on Earth as it is in Heaven."

Two minutes into the practice, a fight broke out in the street below my window. I recognized one of the people involved by her voice, more like a dog's bark than a human voice. The poor woman has one of the most unpleasant and powerful voices I have ever heard. And this time, she was screaming at two or three other people in the street. The argument escalated to a point where I would not have been surprised if it had ended in gunfire. The energy was terrible. And simultaneously, there I was, standing motionless like a tree, inhaling the energy of Heaven and Earth. It did not seem coincidental, and I decided to intervene. I expanded my aura not only upward and downward, but outward toward the street, trying to envelop the situation with the energy of "let there be peace."

I felt as though I were extending an embrace toward them and, in doing so, something terrible entered my aura. This was to be expected: they were fighting, not praying the rosary together. So I held tight to my connection with Heaven and Earth, and also connected the aura of the people involved to the Earth, so that the discordant energy could be neutralized through grounding.

It was an exercise in maintaining internal peace in the presence of terrible energy. I did it with considerable success and, after a few minutes, the fight dissolved.

When the practice ended I felt recharged and at peace. But something stayed with me, because as the afternoon advanced I began to feel worse. I had planned to begin my last practice of the day at 7pm, but an enormous resistance appeared and I spent almost an hour watching YouTube shorts. When I saw it was already 8, I put down my phone and closed my eyes. I observed my body and my mind, dulled by so much noise. I was surprised to find there was no guilt or any other emotion, only white noise. It was a very strange sensation, a suspicious neutrality. I almost felt as though I had no soul.

I began my evening practice, called Old Man Tide Pool. It is designed to purge emotional energies, and it is very powerful. I am doing it for 40 days, and that was night 25. I did it, and purged some energetic blockages that gradually brought me back to a more familiar place: ah, my soul has returned. Some joy, some courage... something!

I finished the practice around 9:15pm and felt I should meditate. I sat down and, the moment I observed my energy, I felt something very unsettling. I asked: "Is there a demon in my aura?" And the answer was: "Yes."

And so began a battle for my mind that would not end until 36 hours later.

The first thing I did was reconnect with the sphere above my head and let it fall over my body, invoking and attuning my aura to the energy of Heaven. Every time I had experienced a similar encounter before, that is what I did, and it always worked to expel whatever malign presence had come to visit. I did it, and felt enormous resistance. My body, possessed, began to growl and heave in a terrifying way. I held tight to the sphere until that wave of dark energy passed. But I could sense something was still there.

"Is there still something in my aura?"

"Yes."

I invoked Jesus Christ and the energy stirred violently. I leapt to my feet, sensing I would need far more power than I could summon sitting down. I spent an hour standing, sweating, invoking the light and expelling demons from my aura.

"IN THE NAME OF JESUS CHRIST, LET THERE BE LIGHT"

"ARCHANGEL SAINT MICHAEL, DEFEND ME IN BATTLE!"

"ONLY LIGHT IS WELCOME HERE!"

The demonic energy passed through me, contorting my body in the most terrifying ways. It growled and resisted the light, clinging to my mind with its claws. It also laughed and mocked me and my invocations. It came in strong waves and, after each one, I would ask again, always with the same answer.

"Is there still something in my aura?"

"Yes."

I thought about calling Joe, knowing there is no darkness that can withstand the magnitude of his light. But at the same time, I felt called to face this alone. I saw in this massive attack a golden opportunity to elevate myself, to clear my mind of vulnerabilities and reclaim the power God wants for me.

Two ideas came to mind to inspire me:

No attack or challenge on the path is too great. But sometimes it can catch you at a moment when you do not feel strong enough to face it. There lies the opportunity for growth: the ego finds itself completely overwhelmed and ultimately has no choice but to ask for divine intervention and step aside.

The second idea is that these malign entities feed on vulnerable minds. If they were now in my mind, it was because there was something in me they could hold on to. I understood it had to do with the fear I had of them. Sooner or later I would have to cross through and release that fear of demons, and this was the opportunity.

It is not possible to return to the light without passing through the darkness. The path to God passes necessarily through hell, and it is there that one faces these things in order to resolve vulnerabilities. And so the truth was restored, coming to me in this moment of need through these words from A Course in Miracles:

Nothing real can be threatened.
Nothing unreal exists.
Therein lies the peace of God.

What I truly am, the Self, is like a cinema screen: whatever happens in the film cannot affect the screen itself. Only from that point of observation can one see that there is truly nothing to fear, that one is eternally safe.

I already knew that "place," I had experienced it on numerous occasions, but my mind still oscillated between that and identification with the character. In the film, Antonio was fighting with all his strength against demons. In the Self, there is no problem, and one can see that everything is in God's hands.

The battle began on Saturday night, and I called on all my strength to deal with the situation. I intuitively embodied the archetype of the spiritual warrior: something like a Jedi.

But my body, after several nights of poor sleep and two hours of fighting on my feet, was beginning to feel very tired. I knew something was still hidden in my aura, and I was terrified of going to sleep and leaving my dreams in its hands. But I saw no other option, so I went to bed, made the sign of the cross, and surrendered to God, falling immediately into a restless sleep.

Fortunately, there were no terrible nightmares or unpleasant experiences. I woke tired, but no more than the days before. I knew something was still there when I noticed an enormous resistance to doing my morning practices. What normally draws me in and gives me peace now repelled me. The battle began again, and I called once more on the Jedi within. But it was not enough, and the struggle was exhausting. It genuinely felt like taking a physical beating.

I remembered reading about Padre Pio expressing similar experiences, unable to understand how a non-physical entity could have the capacity to harm the body. Now I was living it: it is not that these demons take physical form and beat you; the attack occurs at the level of the mind, and the mind controls the body. So when, for example, I invoked Archangel Michael and the light entered my mind, that dark energy expressed itself through my body as though it were its own, contorting in violent ways, emitting growls mixed with macabre laughter. In short, the body was only the battlefield, and the two sides, light and darkness, were fighting for my mind.

Seen from outside, my body was like a marionette in the hands of two quarreling puppeteers. Meanwhile, in my mind, I was using all my tools and watching the malign presence distort them. If I invoked the luminous sphere above my head, what appeared was a black hole. If I directed the celestial substance of the sphere to cover and purify my body, what fell was a cascade of blood. Everything I did was transformed into something horrible.

But on this second day of battle, I began to understand something. What I truly am is love, which is simply the fact that everything is one. By positioning myself as a warrior in the battle, I was separating myself from my enemy, perpetuating the dance of opposites. Seeing this, I began to rise until I could see that, in reality, the demon is part of me, and love unites me to it as much as to Antonio. That is the vision of Christ, and it had such an effect that I thought, after one final shudder, the malign presence had gone.

But no. I still felt as though the sky were overcast. There was no joy, and it was hard to look with love. That is how I spent the rest of Sunday, and my evening practices were not much more effective.

What did happen was that, little by little, the fear began to dissolve. I understood that, for my invocation of God's power to truly take effect, I had to get out of the way. That meant that, in the middle of a demonic attack, instead of positioning myself as a Jedi warrior, I should present myself as defenseless and vulnerable as a child: a child who trusts completely in the protecting arms of his Father.

I also knew that, even though I was now going through a difficult moment, the happy outcome was certain. Everything is in God's hands, and there was not the slightest doubt in me that sooner or later, this passage through hell would end.

So, instead of fighting, I began to relax more and more, and to welcome everything. And I began to discover that true invulnerability lies in total defenselessness. "Do not resist the evil one; but whoever strikes you on your right cheek, turn to him the other also" [Matthew 5:38-39]. These words not only began to make sense, but appeared as the only way that made any sense at all.

On Monday morning, still walking with this dark cloud hovering in my mind, I thought: "Two nights ago, when this began, I saw it as an opportunity to grow. But who was it that thought that? Obviously the ego. So why not ask Joe for help?"

I finally allowed myself to do so and sent him a message explaining the situation.

A few minutes later, two ideas passed through my mind:

"Faith is mental fortitude" and "Say the word and I shall be healed."

These words passed through my mind like a sunbeam and I felt the demonic energy disappear in a breathless exhalation.

I sensed my mind was clean again when, passing in front of a church, I felt the desire to go in. I sat in a pew at the back. It was a large, dark, silent and peaceful space. Three people were praying and I sat down to do the same.

After a while, one of them stood up, knelt, and as he turned around I saw it was my friend Julian. He came over, gave me a hug and asked how I was. I told him it had been one of the hardest weekends of my life. He looked at me and said: "How can I pray for you?"

The question surprised me. No one had ever asked me that. I stayed quiet, searching for an answer. But what came was another question: "Who are you?"

"Pardon?" he said, confused.

"Sorry, I know you are Julian, but that is not what I was asking. I was invoking the light in you, because seeing it reminds me of it in myself."

"Oh, okay!" he said, understandably puzzled. He gave me a hug and left.

I sat back down, and now, grateful and moved, I knew I had made it to the other side.

With all my love,

Antonio
