Antonio de Arcas

A new beginning

Today’s letter opens a new chapter.

For the past ten years I have been asking, again and again, “Who am I?”

Although the true answer cannot be captured by any name or symbol, today I present myself with another name:

When I have a dilemma or a decision to make (especially if it is important) I no longer try to look for the answer on my own. God’s Will is eternal, and therefore the decision has already been made. All I have to do is let go of the illusion that I have the capacity to choose. In doing so, I am removing the clouds that obscure what already is. This is the end of doubt and worry, and the beginning of certainty and trust.

A lived example of this.

This summer I sensed that I needed a new name. Astro Fonda emerged at a time when I was devoted solely to music, and it felt appropriate. But now my main instrument seems to be the pen, and Astro Fonda no longer feels like the right name for my work as a writer.

“What should I call myself?” I began to pray in June.

Names began to flow through my mind.

Antonio Cebada came to me in the middle of the night after repeating that question. I didn’t like it, and I couldn’t fall back asleep. Even so, I respected the option because of the way it arrived.

Antonio Ibrahim also appeared. That is how Betül’s father baptized me the day he met me, during my last visit to Istanbul.

At times I searched actively, but as soon as I noticed myself doing so, I stopped. I no longer make decisions on my own, or at least I try not to. If it is God’s Will that I change my name, the decision is already made, and all I have to do is let go of my preferences, fears, desires, and doubts.

Finally, during my last week at Berklee, the answer came to me while I was meditating at home. The question was open in my mind, and I let go of all desire to control the outcome. Then, at a certain moment, I saw this symbol, and I understood that it was my new signature.

I recognized the initials, and I knew that my new artistic name was Antonio de Arcas.

My vocation now is to learn to live this way.

Letting go of my free will in order to listen to a much deeper and freer Will.

Letting go of my identity as a separate individual in order to recognize myself in you and in everything.

Letting go of fear and worry in order to create with wisdom and trust.

Letting go of the temporary, now, and now again.

Choosing peace, simplicity, forgiveness, happiness.

Letting things happen for me, without planning.

Letting the current of the Tao carry me to where my presence may be useful for its mysterious purposes.

De Arcas carries an implicit promise: there is something valuable in here, perhaps the most valuable thing that can be found in this world. It is within me and, to the same degree, within you, waiting for us to rediscovered it together.

Soon I will begin to announce projects and opportunities to explore all of this in person. For today, the change of name is enough.

Once again, I begin!

With all my love,

A.
