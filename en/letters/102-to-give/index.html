102. To Give

Learning to Live on Earth as in Heaven

These are very beautiful and intense days. The expansion I have chosen for my life is being reflected on every level: my identity, my nervous system, my activity, the way the world responds by bringing me opportunities aligned with that path…

Thank You, Father, for leading me to where I can shine.

Today’s letter is about a shift in perspective that took place yesterday. I had a sales call, and shortly before it, the paradigm of fear and scarcity I had been operating from blew apart. Here I express what I saw on the other side.

After seven years in monk mode, returning to professional life is not so much a return as it is arriving somewhere radically different.

Having inherited from my culture a philosophy of work and money, I now find that those models no longer serve me. My emotional thermostat is very sensitive, and I realize that if I understand money and work through those conventional models, guilt becomes inevitable.

The world’s idea is that we must seek something external to sustain our lives. From that premise, we pursue money as a matter of life and death. But where does that mechanism of seeking come from?

We are human beings. It is a dual nature: being, and human.

Being comes from the Being of God. Being is Spirit. Spirit is infinite and eternal. The very idea of seeking is inconceivable for that which already is everything. What could that which is One possibly seek? What could be lacking to totality?

The human part comes from the evolution of life on this planet, which began with a bacteria adrift in the ocean. That first organism did not contain within itself the source of its sustenance; it had to obtain energy from outside in order to stay alive and reproduce. From that starting point, all life on this planet has always been marked by that basic principle: you must consume other forms of life to sustain your own.

Over eons, life forms became more and more sophisticated until they reached you and me. Our animal inheritance, that set of survival mechanisms, is what we call the ego, and we all come pre-installed with it.

But the animal instrument has become so finely tuned that it is beginning to perceive not only the music, but also the silence. We are starting to realize that yes, our structure is animal; but at a deeper level, what we call “I” is not an animal structure, but the consciousness that inhabits it. We are the Spirit that animates and knows the flesh.

What you experience is animal. That which experiences it is divine.

What we are in essence does not follow the law of nature, but the law of God. That is why nothing this world offers will ever fully satisfy us. God alone suffices.

The animal follows the law of nature:

To give is to lose; to receive is to take.

But above it stands the law of God:

To give is to receive.

The question is: From which law do you want to live? Whom do you serve, and before whom do you kneel? The ego, or the Spirit?

Both systems of thought are incompatible and irreconcilable. One is the negation of the other. We must choose, again and again, in every area of our lives. Not choosing is remaining with the default program.

Now, choosing one does not mean destroying the other. It means reorganizing our values. It means putting each level in its proper place.

“You shall have no other gods before Me” is not a commandment, but a description of how things look when the levels are rightly ordered.

Now, why am I saying all this if I began by speaking about work and money?

Because in the mind that is writing this, the choice has already been made, and now all the things of the world must be translated into the vision of the Spirit.

What does money mean from the perspective of the ego?

It means you can get things out there, along with status and other things considered valuable for survival and reproduction in the jungle.

What does money mean from the Spirit?

Nothing in itself; it is neutral. It is not, therefore, something that makes sense to pursue as an end. It is not that doing so is wrong; it is that it means nothing. To pursue it is, at best, to chase a means. A life organized that way can only feel empty, driven by an ambition without purpose.

And here is where trust comes in: daring to live in this world without a trace of lack, without seeking money as an end or attributing to it a value it does not possess in itself. It is rather a byproduct of an activity that truly seeks something of real value.

As I wrote in my letter “46. Force and Power”:

Force struggles to find the next verse.

Power opens its hands and lets it land.

The same is true of money.

My mind, which is of divine origin, will never feel at ease seeking something it believes it lacks. God asks for nothing and needs nothing external. God only gives.

If I want to live in peace in this world, I must function on Earth as in Heaven.

If I am a child of God, and my nature is the same as my Father’s, then I am here only to give, to extend.

From that perspective, if I am on a sales call, I know I want nothing from that person. I step aside and recognize that I am the light of the world wishing to serve us both. I am there solely to offer all my gifts in service of our shared Being. I come only to give, and the one to whom I give is myself.

That is the perspective from the Spirit, where both people are the same Being. To give is, therefore, to receive.

Now, within the illusion of separation, I open my hands to receive.

And here is the final key that closes the circle: what gives something meaning is its purpose. If, when I receive money, I think only of my own personal satisfaction, then I have collapsed back into the animal law, where one seeks for one’s own survival.

But what if I receive in order to keep giving? I step out of the way (my individual self was never real anyway) and turn my earthly existence into a channel, not a reservoir. Everything that comes to me, I set in motion again in service of life, including my own.

Money is no longer just for me, but to found and care for a family, to invest in my work and in the work of others… in short, to serve a purpose greater than myself.

In my work, I step aside and focus on offering everything I can to the person in front of me.

With money, I manage it so that it may continue to serve in the same way.

As an individual, I disappear from the equation. I remain in the world, but I am not of the world. I am a mirror, not a portrait.

When I step out of the way, what happens to guilt? It has nowhere to cling, no one to attack. In its place shines innocence, gratitude, and an ever more radiant light, because the more I give, the more I am able to give. Now it is a matter of fine-tuning the instrument, allowing my nervous system to adapt to this deeper understanding and identity.

You do not need to seek anything, my dear animal. What you truly need, you already have. You are sustained. You can fully relax. Remain faithful to living in accordance with your true nature, from the integrity of the Spirit you are. Connect with your true purpose, which will always have to do with service, and everything your structure requires will come without your having to seek it.

With all my love,

A.
