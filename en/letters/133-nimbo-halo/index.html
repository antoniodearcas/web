133. Nimbo (Halo)

The world sings if you like to listen.

Last Friday, Nimbo came out, the first single from my new album, Silent Transmission.

133. Nimbo

Lord, how hard it has been!

I composed it six years ago, and I am not exaggerating when I say that getting here has been an odyssey. A journey into the intimately unknown and back.

Nimbo came out on Friday and, the moment I joined the meditation with the community, I began to cry. I saw, like a film, all the stages of the journey, the hardest moments, and how the light would invariably come to take my soul out of the hands of fear. The rest of the half hour was a meditation on how hope is entirely justified when one walks hand in hand with God. There is no obstacle that can resist Him. What for us is a mountain, for Him is not even a feather.

Afterward, this prayer-poem came to express that (trans)personal journey I had just reviewed, inspired by the traditional Jewish prayer "Dayenu" ("It would have been enough"):

If God had shown me, at 21, that I was his beloved son and that there was nothing to forgive
It would have been enough

If he had taken me off the path of engineering and let me fly in the open field
It would have been enough

If he had given me a single inspired song
It would have been enough

If he had opened my heart and healed the wounds kept there
It would have been enough

If he had sent me a beggar with broken legs lying on the pavement to teach me to see Christ in every man
It would have been enough

If he had brought me a good teacher who taught me that my true will is to know God
It would have been enough

If he had brought me A Course in Miracles to teach me to forgive
It would have been enough

If he had restored love and harmony in my family
It would have been enough

If he had healed my voice after four years of constant pharyngitis
It would have been enough

If he had accompanied me through the hell of healing my relationship with food
It would have been enough

If, in my most painful loneliness, he had called me to marriage, brought me Betül, and fulfilled his promise
It would have been enough

If he had placed me in the hands of Jesus and taught me to hear his Voice
It would have been enough

If he had given me the opportunity to record my songs
It would have been enough

If he had dissolved with love the fears that prevented me from sharing them
It would have been enough

If after this no more songs were to come
It would have been enough

If I were to understand nothing more
It would have been enough

If tomorrow this body were to fall
It would have been enough

Because You have given me far more than it would have been reasonable to ask.

How could I not want to give back to You what I have?

I give You my voice
I give You my body
I give You my mind
I give You my marriage
I give You my work
I give You my days

So that all that belongs to the Alpha
may also belong to the Omega

And if after all this
only remained
to love in silence,
to pray,
and to labor,

It would have been enough.

With all my love,

Antonio
