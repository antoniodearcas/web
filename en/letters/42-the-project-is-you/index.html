42. The Project Is You

Yesterday I opened a new path: teaching and conscious community

Yesterday, I activated something new in my life. I opened a teaching space, eight friends showed up, and I dedicated two hours to sharing knowledge that has taken me eight years to acquire—a knowledge that has completely reoriented my life. It’s what took me from being a spiritual vagabond, unclear about who I was or where I was going, to being a pilgrim. Yesterday, I took a big step on what I know is my path, and today I celebrate it with this letter and with gratitude.

I spoke about how to set goals from a spiritual perspective. The spiritual dimension is reality and, therefore, where true power resides. However, it is also a subtle plane, and our attention is so fixed on the attractions of the world that we overlook it. A phrase comes to mind, though I’m not sure if it’s mine or stolen, that captures this well: "trading your soul for a can of sardines."

What we did yesterday was to root our goals in the spiritual dimension—an effort to align our worldly paths with the journey of the soul. In this way, "going to yoga" or "writing my book" cease to be the central projects and become mere tools for working on the real project: the development of your character and your spiritual essence. That's why I called the workshop "The Project Is You."

For me, it was a fascinating experience from which I learned many things. But two things stand out: the sense of fulfillment and gratitude I felt at the end and the initial spark I saw light up—a spark I hope will grow into a bonfire: a community that is conscious and aligned with the same purpose: to live authentically, supporting each other in our journeys of growth and self-knowledge.

Each of us has our own paths and guiding stars, but we all face the same challenges: the same limiting beliefs instilled by our culture, the cultivation of the same virtues, and the overcoming of the same fears, doubts, and prejudices.

We’ve agreed to meet again in a month to see how each of us is progressing and to address any obstacles hindering our paths. When walking alone, a single obstacle can turn into a mountain, causing you to give up or remain stuck for years. But walking together, the sum of each person's experience, knowledge, and light can dissolve the blockage quickly. Because, in truth, there are no obstacles, only dark mirages. But seeing that requires a different perspective. That is the power of the teacher, the friend who truly wants your best, or, in this case, the conscious community.

With all my love,

A.
