The Agent

What is your responsibility, and what is not?

To contemplate Nature, you don't need to go out into the fields. Just observe yourself, without judgment. For a moment, try looking at your body not as "me," but in the way you look at a cloud or a flower.

Who is directing the chemical processes happening in your body right now?

Who is running your organs?

Who is directing the synapses that decide you next thought?

Who heals the wounds in the skin? And the ones in the soul?

Who decided to read this letter?

Who is doing the breathing?

Who decides to relax the forehead and shoulders?

Who is responsible for moving your eyelids?

Who is responsible for everything else you do throughout the day?

The one who chooses the food, cuts it, chews it and digests it, is it always the same agent, or does it change?

Who is at the controls of your life?

Who decides what happens in it? What words you say, who you run into, what you decide to buy, when you decide to pray, when sleep comes over you, when you wake up...

Who draws the line between what is under your control and what is not?

Who decides what you are responsible for, and what you are not?

The ego is the thought that interprets what happens as "I did this, this is me, I am guilty of this, responsible for that, and if I don't do it, it will go undone."

Meanwhile, the heart keeps beating, the photon keeps traveling, the lotus keeps opening, the musician keeps composing, and the baby keeps forming.

With all my love,

Antonio
