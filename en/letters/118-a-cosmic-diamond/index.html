118. A Cosmic Diamond

The ego has a value system, and you don't fit in it

From time to time, a deep truth breaks into the soul and produces a cataclysm in the ego. Last week I lived one of those moments. Here it is.

What is your value as a human being?

As always, there are two ways of seeing it: through the ego, or through the Holy Spirit.

The ego is the thought system built on separation. Once you accept the idea that you are an individual, autonomous being, an entire system grows from that seed, one that interprets what the world is, who others are, and who you are.

The Holy Spirit, on the other hand, is the part of your mind that has never forgotten that you are one with God and with the totality. From that knowing, a completely different system emerges, one that runs counter to the ego in every way.

So when we ask ourselves "What is my value?", the two answers could not be more different.

The ego assigns value based on what favors survival: your wealth, your education, your skills, your health, your capacity to produce or procreate. None of that has anything to do with the truth of who you are. It only speaks to how you interact with the world. Human value, seen this way, becomes highly variable and volatile. Where do the elderly fit in this system? The poor? The sick? At the bottom. The wealthy and influential will always come first, and the rest of us gradually feel ourselves diminished by comparison, until we reach the existential void of feeling useless and disposable.

The inequality we perceive in the world is unresolvable as long as we interpret reality through the ego. It does not matter how wealth is redistributed: some people will always be seen as more valuable than others.

The other day I began reading Magnifica Humanitas, the encyclical of Pope Leo XIV. A few minutes in, I had an experience that brought me great clarity and above all relief about my own value, and therefore about the value of every being. The encyclical places great emphasis on the intrinsic dignity and worth of the human person. But that day I felt small, incapable, useless, and I felt a sharp contrast between what I knew intellectually and what I was actually feeling. So I put the book down, sat with those feelings of smallness and unworthiness, turned my inner gaze upward, and with something like spiritual rage I prayed: "Holy Spirit, I know that my value is already infinite and cannot change, and yet here I am, feeling useless and worthless. If what you say is true, I want to know it now!"

The first thing I was shown was a symbol: a cosmic diamond. Try to hold that in your mind: a diamond the size of the universe.

Immediately after, I remembered perhaps the greatest gift I ever received through Joe, my teacher: we are the original thought of God. The first creative act of God was to create his Son and, through him, the world.

In that moment of need and supplication, the Holy Spirit brought me back to that “place,” and I recognized that my value lives there: I am a creator, not as an artist, but as a participant in the nature of the Son of God, made in his image and likeness. What the Holy Spirit showed me was the Christ in me, the eternal creative principle, the origin of life itself and therefore the essential identity of every living thing. There we are all truly equal, and our value is infinite because it is an extension of the value of God himself.

Seeing that, the feelings of smallness and incapacity were blown away, and one thought passed through my mind: "I want that one on my team!"

With all my love,

Antonio
