Nº 55

The magic of beginnings

On my first concert in 6 years

“And suddenly you know: It's time to start something new and trust the magic of beginnings.” - Meister Eckhart

Last Sunday I gave my first concert in 6 years. After so many deaths and rebirths, I had no idea what to expect. Who was I going to be on stage? How was I going to express everything I’d lived through these past years?

Before this hiatus, most of my songs were an expression of my emotions. If Antonio is a stained glass window, my songs used to speak about its shapes and colors. Emotions, like the images in stained glass, often tell stories—and that’s what I used to talk about. My songs now are all expressions of something more abstract, less tangible, something from another dimension not so easily seen. They are expressions of the light that passes through the stained glass. It’s not so easy to speak about that.

Luckily, the concert took place in a community where light holds a central place. It’s called Kindred, and before I played, Christian, one of the founders, read a beautiful invocation that began like this:

At Kindred, we are here to be attentive to the sacred; to align ourselves with the ineffable. And gathering here at the intersection of arts and mindfulness, we shift our attention from the plans of our busy lives to participate in the divinity that radiates throughout our world.

Afterward, Erin, the founder of Kindred, led a meditation and then passed it on to me.

Silence settled. The room was saturated with a wonderful energy. I turned inward, to listen and know how to proceed, as I hadn’t prepared anything at all. My way of dealing with the insecurities, fears, and perfectionism of the days before had been to plan nothing and turn the concert into a great creative act. I decided to trust in the magic of beginnings—that something new would be born, and it would be good. I chose to trust that the light passing through the stained glass would generously compensate for any lack. I presented myself not with these words, but with this inner posture:

This is me at this point in my evolution. This is the current state of my voice, my guitar playing, my songs with their little flaws, and a treasure inside that I trust I’ll know how to share.

And after a second of silence, I decided to begin by playing Nimbo, as a declaration of intent:

Om

It’s a halo

Om

It’s the light that vibrates within me

A friend

Is the Sun

And it’s you

I played for an hour. My voice, which had been threatening to give out all week, didn’t close up, tired, as I’d been fearing. Instead, it opened up and unfolded more and more until by the end of the concert, it took flight. That’s the moment I want to share with you in this video.

I ended the concert with the song that began my musical path, one many of you may already know in one version or another. I leave you with this live version of If I Was A Bird. In the video, you can feel my joy at playing live again, the relief of having overcome the block with my voice, and after singing the final note, my gratitude to the Love that guides me, heals me, and lifts me.

With that love, filtered through the stained glass,

A.
