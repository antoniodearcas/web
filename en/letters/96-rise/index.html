January 4, 2026

96. Rise

Happy New Year! I’m starting 2026 with a reflection on where I’ve been these past two weeks: a moment of transition, reintegrating into my life in Madrid after four years in Boston. These have been profoundly transformative years, opening me to very different ways of thinking, perceiving, and being. The challenge now is to remain steadfast in this, without being swept away by the current. The Voice of God is silent; the world is noisy. Whom do I choose to listen to?

During my time in Boston, I often compared my spiritual work to entering a cave with a torch. The relative isolation in which I lived often allowed the light to penetrate more deeply into my mind. As a result, the rhythm of catharsis and moments of illumination was frequent. My gratitude for having lived something like that is beyond measure.

But that chapter came to an end, and when I turned the page, I saw that there was nothing else written.

I returned to Madrid, and my work changed radically in form, though little by little I have discovered that not in content.

Before, I went to class to sit in the presence of a saint and speak about how emptiness is not nothing but everything. Life in this world always demands activity, but within me there was a vertiginous stillness and clarity. Everything that appeared in my experience was illuminated by that light, and illusions dissolved like mist when the sun rises.

Now I am in Madrid, and the contrast has been great. My class is no longer a controlled environment. Now it is a Christmas dinner, a crowded shopping mall. I am back in the world, without a structure to hold me. I feel as though I have fallen into a river… or into rapids, rather. It is no longer a surgical work of illuminating the darkness, but rather an attempt to stay afloat.

I knew this was going to happen. In fact, a couple of months ago I already wrote in my letter “86. You Are Mine” that, on a particularly clear day, I had this conversation with my Self:

"Now I see you because I spend fourteen hours a week in the master’s presence. When I return to Spain and lose access to this light, my mind will descend again into the dark valley and fall once more into the river. When I lose the ability to hear you, what will become of me?”

And the answer came:

“Even if you stop hearing me, I will still be here, always, guiding your every action. You are mine.”

After two weeks in Madrid, I search for the meaning of those words constantly; not intellectually, but experientially. When I access the experience of their truth, it is like lifting my head out of the water and taking a breath of air.

As peaceful and loving as my environment here is, it cannot be compared to the stillness and energetic clarity I was growing accustomed to in the constant presence of the teacher. It is not that there are interpersonal conflicts in my circle in Madrid. In fact, there is increasing harmony, joy, and understanding. From a sensory point of view, these have been very joyful days, moving from one celebration to another.

But my mind now goes beyond the surface, beyond the five senses, plunging into the collective mind. It is not that I hear other people’s thoughts (thank God), but I can feel the energy from which each person’s thoughts, emotions and even perceptions arise. At that level, there is no separation between me and anyone else. The supposed barriers that limit and separate one mind from another dissolve. What remains is an energetic river, subtle as silence, yet so powerful that it determines our entire human experience.

With no separation, we merge with the people around us. “Tell me who you walk with and I’ll tell you who you are” sinks its wisdom into this subtle dimension. In general, our minds are utterly chaotic and incoherent, moving from joy to fear, from guilt to anger, from anguish to sadness, like a radio constantly jumping from one station to another.

We generally think these processes are internal and therefore private. But they are not. At the energetic level, there is nothing private. Three years ago, my mind opened to this dimension: I was given conscious access to all that emotional and energetic material, and I am still adapting to living in that murky river that is the collective psyche.

But I do not complain. This is the path, and I would not change it for anything. I suffer when I forget who I am; when I believe I am a sensitive young man adrift in a chaotic world. Antonio is still in the process of maturing, and growth is uncomfortable. But I cling to what I have seen clearly on rare occasions: I am not a temporary appearance; I am Being. What I truly am is already safe, in perfect stillness, luminous and joyful, behind the surface of forms and names. The true meaning of faith is the strength with which I hold on to that identity.

When I remember this, my mind opens to the inner sky, my shoulders relax, and the tension in my face dissolves. I then perceive everyone around me as the same Being. There is no difference between my grandmother, my uncle, and myself. We are all the same light, refracted into all the colors of human experience.

And when I do not remember it (which is most of the time) I simply long for it. And I think of the Russian pilgrim, desperately searching for the answer to the question, “How can I pray without ceasing?”

The answer he finally finds is that the kind of prayer he seeks does not consist merely in repeating formulas and mantras all the time. His unceasing longing is already the constant prayer he is looking for. It lives always in his heart, like a hand raised toward the surface of the river, asking for help.

But one cannot remain there forever. Sooner or later, the division between the one who helps and the one who receives help must dissolve. Unity is unity, and all divisions must be resolved, however paradoxical they may seem.

Coming from that non-dual place, one word has been resonating in my mind with tremendous force and depth. It is a call to take responsibility for my mind, to place it in the hands of the Spirit rather than the ego. When my infinite soul collapses into a mortal body; when my mind accepts as true the stories of fear and despair that the ego repeats, then this word arrives, full of power, love, and trust; the voice of a father teaching his child to walk:

Rise.

With all my love,

A.
