129. You Are NOW

Do you know your true value?

What is it that is valuable?, you say as you fix

on my eye your celestial eye.

What is it that is valuable? And you ask me this?

What is valuable... is you.

This morning I pulled on the thread:

I feel overwhelmed by everything I have to do.

And what happens if you don't do it?

Then the people I work with will think I am sloppy.

And what happens if they think that?

I will feel depressed.

Why?

Because I will feel worthless.

There it is. Stop.

That fear of being worthless is not something that will appear in the future if everything else goes wrong. It is a belief about yourself that is active right now behind the curtain of your conscious mind, influencing everything you do. The pressure you feel to do things comes from there, from that fear of being worthless, from that self that feels insufficient.

Let us question that.

According to the world's value system, our worth is measured by what we have or by what we do. A person is valuable if they have a lot of money, followers, status, connections, an aesthetic body, shiny objects... Or if they do admirable things: they are productive, diligent, athletic, have an interesting and high-impact job... As my friend Juan used to say when we were children, laughing at the values the world was beginning to instill in us: "the most important thing is to be really hot."

Nothing wrong with those values, by the way. In general, they come from legitimate human needs. They are part of the daily bread: having health, a decent livelihood, feeling that we contribute to the world or that we belong to a community. I am not saying, therefore, that the path is to renounce these human realities. What I want to bring to light in this letter is: are you letting those external and highly variable things dictate whether you are valuable or not?

What the world tends to overlook is the value that comes from what we are, and that is where our true dignity and sense of fullness are born.

When we lose contact with what we essentially are, a void is created that nothing in the world can fill. But we try anyway, using having and doing to compensate for that apparently empty, small and insufficient self. From there, we will want to do things well, and that is good. But the fuel behind our actions will be fear; the fear that the world will discover that, in reality, we are not good or valuable or worthy of being loved.

How can we resolve that underlying fear and sense of insufficiency?

We will have to ask the One who knows who we truly are, and what our true value is. We did not create ourselves; God is our Creator, and only He knows perfectly what we are. When we doubt our worth, instead of looking outward and seeking an answer in this mad world, let us turn inward and ask:

Who are we? Show us our value and our identity as You know us.

This morning I asked that question in communion with Betül, and the answer came in this form:

You are NOW.

That answer was not merely a thought. It came with a profoundly liberating shift in perspective: my sense of identity was no longer in my body; rather, I was the sphere of consciousness aware of itself, within which the body, Betül, the entire park and everything else appeared.

You are NOW.

From there I saw that I am no more the body of Antonio than that of Betül, or the tree growing beside us. Our true identity is not different from the eternal NOW that watches the changing world pass by.

From that identity, beyond space and time, one can say: "Before Abraham was, I am."

Betül then rested her head on my shoulder and I felt the love that had inspired that gesture, as well as the love it awakened in me.

You are that love, and there is only one love and it is the love of God.

The question about our intrinsic value was transformed then into:

How much is the love of God worth?

How much is NOW worth?

And a great flock of birds flew past between clouds filled with rain and light.

With all my love,

Antonio
