The Root of Disorder

And how to turn chaos into a path to God

Six years ago I read Walden, the book Henry David Thoreau wrote about his experiment of living in a cabin in the middle of the woods for two years. There I discovered that life could be lived this way: by running experiments with one's own life. I thought then: if I were to make my life an experiment, what would be the hypothesis I'd want to test?

The answer was: "Seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added unto you."

The other day I read this sentence in the book Jesus of Nazareth by Benedict XVI:

"Man is a being immersed in relationships. And if the first and fundamental relationship of man — the relationship with God — is disturbed, nothing else can truly be in order."

There is a very concrete path to God hidden in those words: look at the area of your life that is out of order, and seek there how to restore your relationship with God.

In my own case, I have lived this across several areas of my life.

In intimate relationships.

I used to depend on someone else's love to feel loved. Being single, therefore, felt like an incomplete and unbearable state, almost like being cast out of heaven.

When I began to understand the principle expressed by Ratzinger's words, I started looking inward for the love I had always sought outside. After that journey through the desert, I arrived at the direct experience that the ocean of love that is God was within me. That love was completely unconditional and eternal. Who in this world can offer that?

That freed me entirely from the desire to be in a relationship.

Two days later, my relationship with Betül began.

Now that I am in a relationship more loving than I could ever have imagined, I know that I am not dependent on her love at all. We do not need each other. We are together from love, not in search of love.

By restoring my relationship with God, the area of my life that was romantic relationships naturally fell into a beautiful order.

In my relationship with physical health.

Throughout my life I suffered from illnesses that conventional medicine did not know how to heal. Some were attributed to stress, like my constant stomach pain or the digestive chaos I experienced during my time at Berklee.

Others were labeled "chronic," as if to say: there is nothing to be done here. Among them were asthma and eosinophilic esophagitis.

And in other cases I was simply prescribed medication with no interest in the underlying causes, as happened with the recurring pharyngitis that prevented me from singing, and at times even speaking, for four years.

To bring order to our relationship with God is to bring order to our understanding of what we are, since we are made in his image and likeness.

Today I have no symptoms of any of those illnesses. And if a symptom appears, like a shadow from the past, I immediately recognize that I have forgotten who God is and who I am.

In God there is no illness, and therefore, in me there is none either.

As A Course in Miracles says:

"Illness is a defense against the truth."

As unlikely as it may seem, all of those disorders of the body began to disappear when I restored order in my relationship with the truth about what I am. My mind was healed, and my body followed.

These are just two examples.

My relationship with food was another long journey through the desert before reaching harmony.

My relationship with money is the area where the focus lies right now.

But the principle is always the same:

Before trying to bring order to the world, bring order to your relationship with God.

Then the area of your life that brings you so much suffering becomes, in this light, your path to salvation.

So ask yourself:

In what area of my life would I like there to be more order, more ease, more peace?

Whatever it is, there lies a path to God.

It can all begin with this simple prayer:

"Holy Spirit, help me see this with love."

That, and a minimal willingness to look at the obstacles to love you have been carrying all this time.

This is how the healing process begins, restoring order in your life through your relationship with God.

With all my love,
