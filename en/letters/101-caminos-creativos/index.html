101. Caminos Creativos (Creative Paths)

Have you ever wondered why birds fly in that V-shaped formation?

The main reason is that when a bird flaps its wings, it creates an upward movement of air behind it. If a clever bird positions itself just behind, it can “surf” that vortex of wind and use much less energy. By flying in this formation, each one benefits from the effort of the others, helping one another.

They also take turns leading the formation, so that no one gets too tired.

The difference is not small. According to my research, pelicans or geese that fly in a V can travel up to 70% farther using the same amount of energy than if they were flying alone.

Now, another fact about birds, this time going deeper.

Have you ever wondered how a group of starlings can form a perfectly coordinated cloud in the sky? How do they manage to create such harmonious shapes without colliding?

The key was proposed by a physicist named Rupert Sheldrake, who spoke about something called morphogenetic fields: invisible fields that carry information and guide the way cells, organisms, or even groups behave.

We humans tend to interpret this phenomenon as each bird simply following the ones next to it. We see it that way because that’s how we operate: if we want to move in formation, we watch the person beside us and try not to bump into them.

However, Rupert’s theory proposes something far more beautiful. It suggests that each bird is actually flying independently, following its own inner compass. It is attuned to a morphogenetic field that guides its trajectory, much like iron filings aligning themselves along a field line in the presence of a magnet.

That’s why they all turn at the same time, at exactly the same angle. They fly together, in perfect harmony, yet each one is following its own path.

The reason I share all of this is not because I spend my days thinking about birds, but about the idea of creating a community. The same laws can be applied to a flock of conscious humans flying together in a certain direction. This is especially true and necessary if that direction is inner liberation and the creation of an authentic life.

In every process of spiritual awakening there is an initial phase of feeling very alone; of continuing to live as before, yet feeling very different on the inside. There is an incoherence between your inner life and your outer life. This leads to a crisis, a rupture with your former life.

Then begins a long, difficult, but very beautiful process in which your inner strength starts to win the battle against external pressure. It is a gradual process of understanding who you are, how your mind works, and how your inner world is reflected in the outer one. You realize that for things out there to go well, you have to go inside and bring order there. This is how the hero’s journey begins.

I want to create a community to move forward together in this process to which we’re all being called in this moment of momentous change.

On the one hand, I want it to have the effect of flying in a V formation: the pieces of the puzzle that you have already solved are now available to those who might be stuck. When we come together, that information, which is floating in our shared consciousness, is transmitted silently. Sometimes it is enough to sit in silence with a good friend to remember that, in truth, everything is okay.

Christ, the truth that sets us free, expressed it this way: “For where two or three are gathered in my name, there am I among them.” (Matthew 18:20)

On the other hand, something essential on both the spiritual path and the creative path is to join contexts that support your growth and expansion. We, like the starlings, are always following hidden attractor fields that shape our behavior. The key to growth is to attune yourself to fields that carry you in that direction. That is why the people you surround yourself with are so important. Tell me who you walk with, and I will tell you which direction your life will take.

For the moment, the community itself will be held in Spanish. I wish it could be otherwise right now, and I hope that in time this project may open in English as well. This does not affect Esferas, which I will happily continue to translate for you.

If you want to build a creative life that is a direct expression of your inner world; if you want to devote yourself to developing the potential of your gifts and placing them at the service of your life and the lives of others; if you want to step out of the sphere of fear and live guided by love, inner listening, and connection with God, (and do all of this by practicing your Spanish!) then this community is for you.
