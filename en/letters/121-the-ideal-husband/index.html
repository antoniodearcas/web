The Ideal Husband

Neurotic, scattered, full of shadows.

Going after the princess of Istanbul!

Tomorrow, God willing, Betül's parents will give me their daughter's hand in marriage. Wedding plans are complex, because we live in two different countries and come from two different traditions: Islam and Christianity. Everything is up in the air. Fortunately, the air too is in God's hands and we trust Him to show us when, where and how. For now, one step at a time, and while everything works itself out, we are preparing ourselves inwardly.

A few days ago, at night, sitting alone in front of a bonfire, I began to pray and asked God to make me the perfect husband for her. What I had in mind were all my flaws, my neuroses, my weaknesses and shortcomings, and I asked him to make me the most virtuous and luminous person in the world. But the light entered my mind and illuminated the truth:

"You already are what you are asking for. You are the ideal husband for her, exactly as you are now. The shadows you perceive in yourself are necessary for her process of salvation, just as hers are for yours. How else could she discover true love? Once again I remind you that you are perfect as you are now. Everything is perfect."

Stop and contemplate that, because it speaks of you too. Behind the veils of our mind, the judgments, beliefs, desires and aversions that distort our vision, you stand: a creation of God, as self-evident as you are mysterious. Wanting to be better than one is may sound loving, but it is at its core an attack that comes only from a misunderstanding of what we are. The perennial wisdom says "Know thyself," not "improve thyself."

What is truly and radically loving is to stop fighting what we are. God has a plan, and he did not make a mistake when he wrote your part.

With all my love,

Antonio
