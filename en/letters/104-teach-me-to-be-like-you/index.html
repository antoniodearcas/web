104. Teach me to be like You.

A single thought can change everything

I am spending a few days in Istanbul with my beloved Betül. Our relationship sometimes feels like a monastery of two: we practice A Course in Miracles together and speak almost constantly about God. It is an extraordinary blessing.

We have also been watching the fifth season of The Chosen, so Jesus has been very present in our days.

At a certain moment, while we were walking, a very powerful thought came to my mind. A light appeared, and for a few moments I saw with great clarity. When we arrived home, I wrote this letter.

103. Teach me to be like You

Teach me to be like You.

I was walking with Betül through the narrow streets of Istanbul when this thought passed through my mind. A single thought aligned with the Will of God has unlimited power. Immediately, my perspective changed radically. What I saw through my senses was the same, but its meaning was very different.

If before I was just another pedestrian, looking around and judging everything by appearances, now I was aware that everything around me was part of me, and that its good was my good.

I was also aware that each of my thoughts had an effect on the whole. Upon becoming conscious of that, one cannot help but ask “What kind of thoughts do I want to bring into the world?”

I could also see that I was not the body, but something beyond the world, now looking through Antonio’s body. But that something did not feel strange, as if I had been possessed by something external. That expanded and transcendent Being was more intimate than my compressed, everyday self. I felt truly at home there. It was pure love.

I felt like a gardener entering his garden early in the morning, simply walking around to see how everything was doing. I knew that within me was the power to help whoever needed it. Then the question arose “Whom is it good to help?”

I remembered several sages saying that it is not good to interfere in the destiny of others. Even that it is not good to remove other people’s suffering, since you might be taking away their opportunity to learn.

Then I remembered the years when I constantly lost my voice. I could not speak on the phone for more than 10 minutes without starting to become hoarse. Singing was completely out of the question. Being at Berklee in that condition was very difficult.

One day I was invited to give a concert in a beautiful venue and, in an act of courage, I accepted. Of course, 4 days before the concert I developed pharyngitis. I canceled the concert with great pain, but I also discovered something fundamental in my healing process: this pharyngitis had not been caused by the cold or anything external; it had come from my mind. Every time I thought with fear about losing my voice, I was manifesting the reality I feared.

That week I spoke with Joe, my qigong teacher, and told him that I was so frustrated with my voice that I was considering leaving Berklee. His response was: “That cannot be, come to my office after class.”

Once there, I sat in a chair, he brought his open hand close to my throat and, without touching it at any moment, began projecting over me an energy so high and powerful that it not only healed the pharyngitis, but expelled the entire pattern that had been making me ill.

After that, I never again had pharyngitis or hoarseness. Thanks to that I was able to record my songs, which had been waiting for years, and now I have reopened myself to my destiny as a musician.

Returning to the theme of this letter, which is when God intervenes, in this experience I can see something: I had been studying with Joe for more than a year when he finally helped me in that way. It took less than 10 minutes to heal something that had caused me suffering for years. Why did he not do it before?

I believe it is because I had not yet recognized what the true cause was: my mind. I preferred to think it was something external: the cold, a virus, dry air… that way I did not have to take responsibility for my thoughts.

When I finally recognized that something in my way of thinking was causing my illness, I made an inner decision. I did not know clearly how to change, but I knew that I did not want to continue thinking in that way. And perhaps it was that decision that opened the door. A few days later, the miracle came.

From then on, every time a thought of fear about losing my voice passed through my mind, I would declare “In the name of Jesus Christ, I cancel that thought. I will sing.”

All of this has helped me better understand why Jesus, before performing a miracle, would ask “Do you want to be healed?” Not even He, whose will is the Will of God, can oppose our will. If we do not want to heal, nothing can help us. Real healing occurs in the mind, not in the body. The body simply tends to manifest what the mind holds.

After performing a miracle, Jesus would also say “[…] and sin no more.” Perhaps what He was saying is “I have freed you from the effects (the illness) and also from the cause (your thoughts of illness). You are free. Do not choose again the patterns of thought and action that led you there in the first place.”

There is no amount of miracles that can help us if we are not willing to make that inner change. Now, if there is within us even a small willingness to change, to let go of our attachment to negativity and choose peace in its place, then our will will have aligned with the Will, and our small strength will be supported by the strength of God.

That change begins with a single truly loving thought. One that helped me greatly during that time was “I am not a victim of the world I see.” A thought that came to help me today was “Teach me to be like You.”

With all my love,

Antonio
