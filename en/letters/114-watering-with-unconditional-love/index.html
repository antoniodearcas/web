114. Watering with Unconditional Love

I'll admit judgment doesn't really work that well.

As I let go of layer after layer of what I am not, I see more and more clearly that what I am is an open field, a sky that nourishes with its light the garden where all beings appear. My work, then, is to be that light and, when clouds appear, not to forget that behind the clouds, the sun is still shining just the same.

All things are, at every moment, the best they can be. If they could be better, they would be. And all are good because they are a manifestation of life itself. Life can take forms with greater or lesser grace and harmony, but that judgment is arbitrary, relative, and irrelevant if what we seek is the Truth.

Truth, if it does not include unconditional love, cannot be the Truth.

Today, during a long and spontaneous meditation, this loving understanding turned toward me, and I saw myself under that benevolent light. Once again, I was shown that the only one who judges me is myself. Seeing myself this way, I understood why I deserve to be loved completely, and why there is no sense in blaming myself for the things I did or did not do.

I am exactly what I am at every moment, and I could not be any other way. Like a rose in the process of opening, perfectly fulfilling the role it must play at each point in its unfolding. There is no sense in blaming the rose for not having opened fully yet, nor does it make sense to demand it or force it to do so. Any attempt to force distorts and hinders the natural process, and will never reach the fullness that only love can inspire.

The best thing you can do with that rose you are is to love it, appreciate what it is in this moment, and give it the nourishment it needs to grow in the healthiest way possible.

God alone is responsible for the results. You are simply responsible for choosing the most favorable conditions. And that means choosing to sustain yourself in the energy of unconditional love, moment to moment, so that it is that energy that nourishes you and encourages you to open up to all that you are.

With all my love,

Antonio
