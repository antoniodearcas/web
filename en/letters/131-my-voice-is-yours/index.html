My Voice Is Yours

Chronicle of a concert-liturgy

I did the sound check while the first people were beginning to enter the auditorium. When I finished, I returned to my sit, and turned inward.

Am I nervous? Not really, but there is uncertainty, I don’t know what to play. I’ve only been given ten minutes. I thought I would have more time. Normally I guide a meditation for at least 20 minutes and then play my songs. How can I condense it all? Do I skip the meditation and give a regular concert? There’s only enough time for two songs, which ones do I play? Nimbo, without a doubt, and the other one?…

This is how my mind was going as the room filled up. 400 people took their seats, laughing, greeting one another. Otman, the presenter, took the microphone, greeted everyone, and explained that the first act of the day was going to be a choreography by the team of volunteers who were helping with the event. “Music, maestro!” and the conversation of 400 people was swallowed up by party music playing at full volume. People shouted and applauded. I kept my eyes closed, searching for the calm and the Voice that had the answer to my questions. Otman then invited everyone to dance, and I watched the entire room stand up and dance around me. I remained seated, meditating. My ego said, “What are you doing! Get up and dance! Don’t you know how to enjoy yourself? You’re out of place, they’re surely looking at you and judging you: ‘Look at that guy, how spiritual…’”

Ignoring that voice afraid of social rejection, I continued sinking inward, toward silence and stillness, now with the added difficulty of being seated in the middle of a sort of nightclub. But there it was, perfect stillness, the endless space that loves us from the depths. I immersed myself there. The noisy and agitated world around me now seemed distant, as if I were diving 10 metres deep and looking at the sky through the surface. Inside me, silence.

When I arrived there, I prayed: “Holy Spirit, I don’t know what to play, I don’t know what to say or what to do. But You do. I give You my hands, my voice, my body, my brain, I give You my mind. This concert is Yours. I am Yours.”

And my mind became calm, the questions dissolved, and I calmly waited for my turn.

The music ended, Otman took the microphone again and said that something very special was coming now. He invited everyone to close their eyes, and brought their attention to their breathing and the present moment. I looked at him, he was looking at me and gestured for me to come up onto the stage. I took my guitar, went up, and standing beside him I began meditating too. In silence, he placed the microphone in front of me, gave me a pat as if saying “they’re yours,” and stepped down from the stage. My mind was in complete silence, my body in the wu chi position.

Remain with your eyes closed.

As you close your eyes, become aware that you are no longer in this room, you are in the open space of your consciousness. Within it appear the sensations of the body, different perceptions, my voice, everything floating in a dark space without limits.

Now, above your head, a sphere of energy appears.

This sphere represents, and is, All that is.

The answer to all questions, the ultimate end of every longing, everything you could possibly need, everything is in the sphere.

It has received many names throughout history: Yahweh, Brahman, wuji, God…

It is the source of your existence and, therefore, is part of you. Now we invoke it, and we are going to allow ourselves to be attuned by it. The divine substance of the sphere begins to pour over you and first floods your head, filling it with light. Your head shines like a lighthouse illuminating the room, the world, the entire universe. You are the light of the world.

The substance continues descending through the neck, shoulders, arms. It descends through the chest, flooding the heart with stillness and calm. The peace of God is in your hearts.

It continues descending through the abdomen, the pelvic area, the legs, all the way to the feet, and enters the earth.

We are all now attuned to the sphere.

The room was in perfect silence. I opened my eyes and saw that some people were crying. I closed them again, and I knew that what I am was permeating the entire room. I was the presence of light, falling through everyone’s bodies from the sphere. There was a total certainty that everyone was receiving it, even though each person registered it according to their own capacity.

I adjusted my guitar properly and began to play Nimbo.

I watched my body play, at first with its hands trembling. I heard my voice sing the first melodies with a certain care, as if feeling its way through the terrain of my body. Then, seeing that everything was in its place, the voice began to sound more confident and powerful. When I reached the chorus, the machinery of the body and the Machine Operator were one, and I felt that I was neither of them. Everything was happening by itself.

When the song ended, I raised one hand as if saying “hold the silence.” Nobody applauded, it was obvious that doing so would have stirred the waters.

Now, the divine substance, which continues falling like a waterfall through your entire body, begins to spread across the floor of the room.

The sphere is your Being, and now, by merging our energy with that of the others, we see that in reality it is the same thing, there is only one Being, there is only one sphere, and all our minds are merged in the mind of God. We are in communion.

Knowing that there is no separation between your mind and that of the others, and knowing that each one of your thoughts has an effect on the others, I invite you to bring a loving thought into the room, something that you would like to give as a gift to all your brothers and sisters gathered here.

The intensity of the joy multiplied instantly. More people crying, knowing themselves embraced by 400 souls finally recognizing that there is no separation.

In that silence saturated with light, I began to play Known = Loved.

Any insecurity that may have been present at the beginning had already dissolved here. In a state of total surrender, I felt and heard my voice fly freely throughout my entire range. It sounded with such power that, at the climax of the song, I felt that my voice was not being produced by my body, but was an emanation of the very space of the room.

I finished the song feeling my instrument reach its limit:

I am the Love that howls in the night
I am the Love that longs for your eyes
I am the Love that holds you in spite of all the ways in which you feel you don’t deserve to be loved

After that final and long verse, sung a cappella, silence.

My entire body reverberated with such intense energy that I had to project it downward, like a grounding wire redirecting a lightning bolt.

10 seconds of silence, tears, and faces of recollection.

You may open your eyes.

I had planned to play one more song, but the entire room erupted in applause. Whistles. Everyone on their feet.

I remained as if still meditating, receiving the intensity of that ovation. I moved away from the microphone, bowed, and thanked them. I raised one hand pointing toward the sky, redirecting the applause toward the One who deserved our gratitude.

The applause continued, and I placed my hand on my heart to thank them again. There, beneath my shirt, I felt my crucifix. I took it out, and it shone like a star on my chest in the light of the spotlights.

Nacho, the organiser of the event, came up onto the stage and said “when I see Antonio I see God”

He gave me a hug. The applause continued. I thanked them again and stepped down from the stage.

On my way to my seat, several people approached me to give me hugs. I gave them hugs, happy. As I embraced their bodies, I brought my attention to their hearts, and then silently showed them the context of their existence, the infinite Being that is in the whole universe.

After several minutes like this giving hugs like these, I reached my seat, sat down, closed my eyes and gave thanks.

Only then did I realize that at no point had I introduced myself. What a disaster! my ego said.

But I smiled thinking: “Well, I suppose this wasn’t about me. And perhaps that’s better this way, the Holy Spirit knows what He is doing”

And so it was. Not saying my name meant that the people who really wanted to get to know me and my work came up to me. I spoke with several of them, who said things like

“Thank you from the heart, what you did went right through me” or “It had been a long time since I had experienced the presence of the Holy Spirit. Thank you!” or “Thank you for stepping out of the way.”

Another greeted me saying “The voice of God!”

Inside me, I continued holding the finger pointed upward.

Telling all of this is an exercise in daring to express with true openness how I experience life. I have been given an immense gift, one that loses its value when hidden, and multiplies when given away.

Now I understand better what I have been looking for: birds sing not to achieve anything, but because they are what they are.

I am what I am, and I must sing my truth. Let nothing remain unsaid.

With all my love,

Antonio
