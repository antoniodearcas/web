End-of-Year Reflection: Spirituality

5 questions about each area of your life to end the year with clarity

Over the past few months, I’ve spent the end of each Sunday reflecting on my week and planning the next one. Instead of starting with a blank page, I’ve used a series of questions I designed to systematically and quickly cover every area of my life.

The first time I responded to these questions, it took about an hour. When I finished, it felt as if a dark cloud had lifted. I realized that much of the overwhelm I was feeling came from not having a clear sense of where I stood. That lack of clarity opened the door to anxiety, which felt like a constant, low-magnitude fear whispering, "something bad is going to happen," without specifying what.

Answering these questions gave me clarity about where I was in life. I saw which areas deserved celebration and which needed more attention. I also recognized that life is a continuous process of growth, and it’s unreasonable to expect everything to be perfect right now. To truly accept my life as it is, I needed to view it not from the "ant’s-eye view" of day-to-day tasks but from the "eagle’s-eye view," flying high above.

Now that we’re nearing the end of the year, I’ve redesigned these questions and decided to share them here. If you’re looking for clarity about your life’s evolution, this process may help. I’ve divided the questions into different life areas, with five questions for each, which I’ll share across upcoming letters alongside personal reflections.

Let’s begin at the top: spirituality.

1. What brought me peace this year, and how can I continue cultivating it?

2. How has my understanding and relationship with God / the Universe / Spirit changed this year?

3. How would I define my life’s purpose right now? What is my guiding star?

4. What is my intention for this year? What is my spiritual goal?

(This could be developing a specific virtue, such as calmness or diligence. It could also involve bringing more awareness, love, or forgiveness to an area of your life that is out of harmony. Or it could be the development of a new habit or abandoning an old one.)

5. Which people in my life help me stay connected and aligned? (Consider expressing your love and gratitude to them.)

REFLECTION

"But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you." (Matthew 6:33)

This year has been the first in which I began to consciously experience the truth of this promise from Jesus. On several occasions, after committing to take responsibility for a particular aspect of my life, help arrived almost immediately—without looking for it, with a smile and a willingness to support me. Let me share the most dramatic and clear example of this:

A couple of months ago, I had a major revelation about my musical path. I saw myself completely free of fear and doubt, and what I saw was a clear path where I fully developed my gifts and shared them with the world. My response to that vision was to fully commit to that path and never again allow fear or doubt to divert me. Still in that state, I wrote, “30. It Was Always Music, and It Always Will Be.”

That same afternoon, a new friend and mentor came into my life and offered to help me in any way she could to fulfill that vision. I met a young man, a student of film score composition, who offered to help with my new song by composing and recording the orchestral arrangements. A little later, I met two other students, specializing in audio engineering and music production, who offered to record any songs I wanted using their analog equipment.

All of this happened in just a few hours. I don’t know if this is a typical day for other Berklee students, but it certainly wasn’t for me. Months can go by without meeting someone I connect with deeply enough to want to collaborate. Yet, in a single afternoon, four new people came into my life with the intention of supporting my journey and my music, meeting all the needs of where I currently am in my evolution.

At the end of the day, I shared everything—the revelation and the newfound help—with B. In her usual calm and wise manner, she said to me, “Today, you aligned yourself with God’s Will.”

God’s Will is the will of the Universe. Imagine the evolution of the universe as a great river. Developing your spirituality is about stopping the fight against the current and learning to go with the flow. Letting yourself go in a normal river might end disastrously—with falls or drowning. But this is not just any river. This universal river is infinitely intelligent and loving. It knows you and loves you infinitely more than you know and love yourself. Aligning with it means letting yourself be carried toward order, harmony, love, beauty, health, and fulfillment.

The way the river shows us we’re not aligned with its flow is through friction: suffering. It’s not the river that causes the friction. The river simply is, and it has been flowing for eons. The only cause of the friction is our ignorance, which shouts, “Swim that way!” without considering the direction of the current. Developing our spirituality means learning to sense the direction of the current and letting ourselves align with it. It’s about learning to flow with life instead of against it. Life will then bring everything necessary to sustain itself, echoing what Jesus said, though in different words.

So, let’s get to work—and with joy! If you’re not already doing this in your own way, I encourage you to give yourself this gift of reflecting on your life at the end of this year. The Universe is serving it to you on a silver platter. More episodes with additional questions on other areas of life are coming, and the invitation is to dedicate a few minutes each day to this practice. Here are today’s questions again:

3. How would I define my life’s purpose right now? What is my guiding star?

4. What is my intention for this year? What is my spiritual goal?

(This could be developing a specific virtue, such as calmness or diligence. It could also involve bringing more awareness, love, or forgiveness to an area of your life that is out of harmony. Or it could be the development of a new habit or abandoning an old one.)

With all my love,

A.
