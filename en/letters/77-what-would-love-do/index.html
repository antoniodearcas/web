77. What would Love do?

A vision of our divine identity

“God is a circle whose center is everywhere and whose circumference is nowhere.”

Alain de Lille

The mind of God is the void that contains the totality.

My mind is a sphere floating in that void.

Inside the sphere lies the world I experience.

The world I experience is perfectly consistent with my mind.

The world and my mind are, in fact, the same thing.

Everything that happens in my world is an expression of my own mind.

If I change my mind, I change my world.

At the center of the sphere is my body.

The body expresses what happens in the mind, like everything else.

I am the mind, not the body.

My body appears within the sphere, just like the computer on which I write.

Both are forms within my own mind, and I am no more one than the other.

Nothing can happen in my world that my mind has not requested.

God, the creative Light, passes through my mind and creates what my mind asks for.

Each of our thoughts generates the world we know.

The freedom God gives us in this sense is total.

We can generate whatever we want within the sphere: abundant lives or barren deserts, heavenly or hellish experiences. We can create aligned with love, or from fear.

There are no rules beyond those we have accepted in our own mind.

Nothing can oppose our will.

The rules that guide our life are individual to each mind, although the ego is universal and we all share its beliefs to a greater or lesser extent. All spheres are equal in essence, though their content varies according to what each mind has chosen.

The sphere is innocent.

You are innocent.

What happens within the sphere cannot affect the sphere itself, just as what happens in the movie cannot affect the screen.

There are two vital orientations:

First, we live looking outward. Here, identification with the body is total, and everything that is not the body is interpreted as something external to us.

Then, we look inward. Here, identification with the body begins to soften, and our identity with the world begins to be revealed. Everything in the world then becomes a mirror, offering a reflection of the state of our own mind.

When this change of orientation (conversion) begins to occur, one of the first signs is coincidences. The world out there suddenly seems connected with the content of our mind. Truth begins to dawn: I am not a body, I am a creative mind, and the world I see is a product of my mind.

By looking inward in this way, we begin to recognize that all the suffering we experience has its root solely and exclusively in our mind—in how we interpret what happens, in what we ask of God with every thought. Every thought is a prayer. To fear or to worry is to pray for what we do not want to occur. As we begin to recognize this, we start seeking minds more aligned with love, so that they may teach us to recognize the errors of our own mind. We begin reading books, turning to teachers, still looking outward for the source of truth.

But the truth we seek in the world actually comes from our own mind. Everything we experience appears in our own mind. Truth does not come from within the world; truth comes from opening to what lies beyond our mind: the Presence of God, the original void, the unified field from which the sphere of our mind arises.

Little by little, we discover that within our mind there is the world, but also the totality of God. Both are accessible, and they can be compared. By comparing our beliefs with the Reality of God, we can identify whether they are true or false. The mechanism within our mind that performs this comparison is the Holy Spirit. We all carry it by nature, and we can ask for its help at any time. God placed it there as a response to our belief in separation, to undo the error when we became ready to look at it.

Regardless of what happens inside it, the sphere remains intact, unchanging in the eternal mind of God. The sphere is your true identity, before you accepted other identities based on separation. The sphere remains God’s creation, and shares all His qualities. The sphere is Christ. Jesus was a body, a mind, a sphere perfectly aware of His true identity. The true identity of Jesus is also our true identity, and he still offers us this recognition. He is still present. He is our very presence. Christ is our identity before the ego arises. The ego identifies no with the eternal sphere, but with the passing contents of the sphere. Christ is the screen; the ego is the belief that we are the movie.

Within the sphere, every instant offers a decision to be made. Despite the apparent variety, the decision is always the same: do I follow the ego, or Christ? Do I follow fear, or love? Do I follow desire, or my true will? Do I take a step toward hell, or toward heaven?

What happens within the sphere can, therefore, be a product of the ego, or a creation of God. They are, of course, not in the same order of reality. The products of the ego are like clouds, passing. The creations of God like the sky, ever-present. The clouds can obscure the sky, but not change it in any way.

The power of decision is still ours. We only need to learn to identify where the thoughts that guide us come from. The thoughts of the ego come from fear, from lack, from separation. The thoughts of the sphere, of Christ, of God, are constant, silent, peaceful—they come from love.

Therefore, in every decision where we do not know what to do, we can ask:

What would love do?

Asking this is easy, and the answer will come immediately. What may require some training is learning how to listen. We must be willing not only to ask for the answer, but also to receive it. The ego fears the answer because it would expose its falseness, and thus it uses all sorts of strategies to conceal that loving voice.

Another way of asking the same is to say: “Thy Will be done.” By invoking the will of God, we are bypassing the apparent limitations of our mind and asking the sphere itself to choose. The sphere is you. The will of God is your true will: that you be happy, that you exist in a state of peace, recognizing that everything is given to you, that nothing is lacking, that you exist in the love and fullness of God and not in the world of fear and scarcity of the ego.

To recognize that we are the sphere is the birth of Christ in the innermost depths of our being. It is the shift from identification with the body to identification with the Spirit. God appears in our mind as our true identity: free, loving, infinite, immortal. To remember this is to awaken from the dream of being trapped in a world ruled by fear and death. To accept this transcendent identity is to heal our mind and return Home, which is the awareness that the love of God is all there is.

There will be opposition. The ego is clever and will always resist with threats of doom and death. But death is not real. Everything that appears within the sphere has a beginning and an end. Not so the sphere. “There never was a time when you or I did not exist,” said Krishna. The sphere was created by God as an extension of His love. The sphere is His Son, His Daughter, His Creation, His joy, His only treasure, His Self. The sphere is you.

God holds us in an infinite embrace, offering His peace at every instant. But God is patient, and we are free. He will not attack our ego and impose His loving will. It is up to us to recognize the devastating effects of following the ego, and to choose instead to follow the truth. God is always ready to align our world with His peace, to the extent that we are willing to accept it.

This change in understanding is so radical that I do not expect you to believe any of it. Buddha said: “Do not believe anything I say. Test my words as a goldsmith tests gold.”

If you’ve reached this point, thank you for reading. Now, don’t ask yourself if any of this is right or wrong, true or false, just be aware of how you feel, of what this has given you at an energetic level.

As for me, I write only to reinforce something I see right now. I write to align my mind with a perspective that has the power to undo all my forms of suffering. I write as a response to my prayer. It sometimes come through songs, sometimes conversations, sometimes through silence, and today through writing.

This letter has appeared after nearly 600 days of practicing A Course in Miracles, a course in learning to forgive and to release the accumulated negativity in our minds. It’s a course on clearing clouds. This morning, in a moment of need, I asked: “What would love do?” I received the answer, which clearly went against the wishes of the ego. I felt the conflict and asked for help. I then heard my own voice say: “I am here.” This time I recognized that this simple phrase did not come from within the sphere, from my supposed limited mind. It was the sphere itself, and what lies beyond, that said: “I am here.” My body relaxed, I felt embraced, the conflict disappeared, and I felt the peace that God always offers me and that I so rarely accept.

A while later, the conflict returned, and I prayed again. This time I saw that this conflict and the thought system from which it arose were in my mind because I had wanted it so. The answer to my prayer was: “This will disappear when you completely withdraw your support from this conflict-driven thought system. Commit fully to what you know is true, and your commitment will leave no room for this falsity to affect your mind.”

Immediately, I opened the computer and wrote the following lines:

The truth about me is that I did not create myself.
I am not what my ego says I am.
I was created by God, and I remain as God created me.
Nothing else is true about me.
I am a Child of God, an extension of God’s love.
I am not a body.
I have a body and I can care for it with love, but for that it is not necessary to identify with it.
My mind transcends the body.
My mind has no limits and is therefore one with the mind of God.
My mind holds only the thoughts I think with God.
The voice of the ego is not real, it was not created by God, and therefore it does not exist.
It is an invention of confusion, and any collaboration with it sustains its apparent existence.
To resist the voice of the ego is to collaborate with it.
To oppose something is to admit its reality.
The ego does not exist and has no power over me, except that which I give it.
If I do give it power, then it has the power I have conferred upon it.
I no longer give it power.
I commit fully to the truth.
I return my mind intact to God, from whom it never separated.
Only God is true.
Truth is indivisible, and therefore the truth of what I am cannot change.
I am still as God created me.
I renounce collaboration with any other thought system that seeks to limit what I am.
I have no limits, because the love of God has none.
I am free.
No voice that says otherwise is real.
I lack nothing and I desire nothing.
I am free, moment by moment, recognizing my divine identity, the Self in which this entire world appears, changes and disappears.
But I do not change, I remain as God created me.
Let there be light, and may my true identity be revealed and accepted in this instant.

I am here.

With all my love,

A.
