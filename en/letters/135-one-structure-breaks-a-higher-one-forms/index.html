One structure breaks, a higher one forms

How marriage, and other changes, have been changing my identity

In the space of a month, I have gotten married, started releasing my songs, and half-moved to Istanbul. I have also suddenly understood the importance of character development, which has opened my eyes to that dimension with unusual clarity: ouch.

Let's talk about how to navigate the identity shift that the inevitable changes in circumstance bring with them.

When something ends, whether it was good or not, we humans suffer. We confuse our circumstance with our identity, and when the circumstance ends, the associated identity feels threatened.

An example:

I am now someone's husband. That identity is new, although it has been a slow-burning change. I met Betül three years ago, but I knew I was going to marry her four years ago.

This new identity is so deep that it includes another person within it. It is a change that encompasses my whole being, like someone who becomes a monk.

In the process of becoming Betül's husband, I have experienced the same pattern again and again: my new shared way of life reveals that part of my identity no longer works: the Antonio-lone-wolf, the Antonio-who-doesn't-make-the-bed, the Antonio-who-only-thinks-of-himself, the Antonio-who-lets-himself-be-carried-away-by-melancholy... Blessed be marriage, which calls us to leave those cages behind.

With each of those identities, the same thing happened:

the mirror of the relationship makes me aware of a destructive identity,

I feel shame and guilt,

I project that negativity outward and that makes me feel attacked,

the identity defends itself by justifying itself,

at some point the spiritual alarm goes off: I seek the truth,

I see that what is calling me to change is not only my circumstance, or a beautiful Turkish girl: it is the healing voice of God himself,

I stop projecting my pain,

I take up my cross,

I see that the one carrying it is Christ in me,

I choose to stop crucifying him,

I hand the guilty identity over to the fire of innocence,

tremors in the shadow,

tectonic plates grinding beneath the surface of consciousness,

the light of Christ shines through the clouds,

the trumpets of redemption sound,

calm

something new is born: the Antonio-who-loves-constant-company, the Antonio-who-makes-the-bed-as-prayer, the Antonio-who-remembers-at-the-shop-what-Betül-mentioned-had-run-out, the Antonio-who-chooses-to-pull-himself-free-from-the-dark-hand-of-melancholy-to-bring-hope-into-Betül's-world.

One structure breaks, a higher one forms.

With all my love,

Antonio
