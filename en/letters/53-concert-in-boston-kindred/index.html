Concert in Boston: Kindred

After a 6-year break, I’m returning to the stage

My last official concert was in December 2019. I lost my voice halfway through the show, and since then, I kept losing it so often that I stepped away from the stage and the studio. A year ago, I finally resolved the internal issue that kept making me sick again and again, and I haven’t lost my voice since. This Sunday, I’ll be giving my first official concert (I say official because I have played two or three songs here and there for friends and family). And I’m fortunate that this return is happening in a space that couldn’t be more fitting.

It’s called Kindred. The first time I attended one of their events, the founder, Erin, welcomed my friend and me with a hug and a cup of tea. We walked into a cozy living room where about 20 people were already gathered. Erin invited us to share our “state of soul”. Then she led a Metta meditation, which follows this structure:

May you be happy.

May you be healthy.

May you be safe.

May you live with ease.

Finally, she passed around watercolors, pens, and notebooks, and introduced the artist—a wonderful vibraphonist—who played for about 45 minutes while the rest of us painted, wrote, or meditated.

Kindred is a beautiful example of community—a space of practice for a life rooted in communion, contemplation, and creativity. It’s the perfect place to return to the stage, especially after all the inner transformations I’ve gone through in these past years.

If you’re in Boston, come spend Sunday evening with me. Here are the details:

When: March 23, from 7:00 to 9:00 PM

Where: Vivid Oblivion, 288 Norfolk Street

To reserve your spot, we kindly suggest a $15 contribution, which will go toward supporting our artist and the space. No one will be turned away for lack of funds. You can pay at the door or through the Eventbrite link HERE.

With all my love,

A.
