76. Invocation

You are breathing.

There’s a gentle tingling in your feet.

Your shoulders relax, now.

Your forehead too.

There is air all around you; you can feel it in your nose as you inhale.

There is a sound you weren’t aware of a moment ago.

You are alive.

You are the awareness in which your body, your mind, and the world appear.

You are safe.

Let there be joy on your face.

Let there be stillness in your heart.

Let there be light.

I am here.
