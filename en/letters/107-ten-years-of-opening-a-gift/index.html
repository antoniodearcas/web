107. Ten Years of Opening a Gift

On the day I was born again

Today’s letter arrives a day late. After Friday’s concert, I needed to rest. What happened was mysterious and wonderful. My hands lit up like neon. Catharsis, invisible embraces, silence. A harmony that did not distinguish between strangers and friends. I will speak more about this event once I have digested it.

Today I bring a reflection on something else that has taken place during this week of the spring equinox: the tenth anniversary of my second birth.

This week marked the tenth anniversary of the epiphany that lifted me out of depression, atheism, solipsism, and, in a sense, out of the world itself. It revealed to me that the essence of reality is not matter, but love.

That leap in consciousness happened at the moment I realized I was trapped in a mental cage of my own making. My way of seeing the world made sense; it had its own internal logic. And yet it was incompatible with happiness.

This confronted me with the question every mind must face on its path toward wisdom: what if logic and reason fall far short of grasping reality?

As Blaise Pascal wrote, “The final step of reason is to recognize that there are an infinite number of things which are beyond it.”

Then I saw that there was only one way out:

“God, if you are there, please help me.”

What followed was an opening of my mind to something completely astonishing: my presence was not limited to my body, it was everywhere.

It was as if all this time I had been a (seemingly) lost piece of a puzzle, looking toward my body and trying to decipher my meaning on my own. Now, suddenly, as my mind opened, I looked outward and recognized that I was actually perfectly placed within the rest of the puzzle, forming an image far greater than what I had previously thought of as “myself.”

I discovered that I am not the missing piece, I am the whole image of the puzzle.

That revelation was beyond the reach of logic and reason. And yet, for the first time, I felt happiness, fuller than ever before. The sense of relief was total.

That silent Presence said to me:

“You have been lost, but now you have returned home. You are my son, and you are infinitely loved exactly as you are. There is nothing to forgive.”

That message, the very essence of the parable of the Prodigal Son, was engraved in my heart.

After four hours dissolved in that ocean of love, the Presence hid itself again behind the veil of perception, and my mind gradually collapsed back into the individual self.

I understood nothing. But I knew I had been given an immense gift, and I have spent the last ten years trying to accept it.

This week I read a sentence from A Course in Miracles that captures this process well:
“You do not need God’s blessing; you have that forever. But you do need your own.”

There lies the key to everything. God’s love for you is total and unchanging. Nothing we do can alter it in the slightest.

So what is the problem?

That our sophisticated primate mind still hasn’t realized this. It still believes it lives in the jungle, when in reality it lives in the Love of God.

That is why there can be two births within a single lifetime:

A birth of the flesh, which gives rise to a life in the jungle. There, the law is survival through attack, and the end is death.

The second birth is of the Spirit, which gives rise to life in God, whose law is Love and has no end.

That day, at the beginning of the spring of my twenty-first year, I was born in the Spirit. Since then I am no longer only the son of my parents, but also the son of Love itself.

My origin is no longer only in the past and in my ancestors. My origin, and yours as well, is now and always.

What am I?

Mortal or immortal?
Limited or infinite?

Between those irreconcilable extremes my sense of identity has oscillated over these last ten years. But deep down I know what I know.

The gift has already been accepted.
I am simply opening it, little by little.

With all my love,

Antonio
