47. El Cielo Que Hay En Mí (The Heaven Within Me)

Today I bring a song

Today I bring a song.

Last week, one of my mentors encouraged me to participate in the Tiny Desk contest. This platform records concerts at its offices in a small, laid-back format. Some of the biggest bands in the world have performed there—I remember enjoying one by Wilco and another by Coldplay, for example.

Every year they hold a contest where emerging artists are invited to record home videos in a similar style. The winner is invited to the offices to record a concert, among other things.

I feel that it's not yet my time to share what I'm working on. That moment will come. But I have a good song, and this is a good opportunity. So here it goes: “El Cielo Que Hay En Mí.” I'll tell you more about it in due time, there’s a lot to share.

Enjoy!

With all my love,

A.
