All Reflections Speak of the Sun

The letter for today is as profound as it is practical. Every day we experience different expressions of love, yet our busy minds rarely penetrate deeply enough to reach what lies behind them. Today I want to unwrap those gifts—those small, everyday loving gestures—and contemplate the jewel within.

If we contemplate a tiny reflection on the wall, we can hear it speaking to us of the totality of the Sun.

Now think of all the expressions of love you receive throughout the day: a smiling “good morning” (someone is happy to see you), a driver who lets you go first, a friend who writes to you, a dog that comes over to greet you, a hug, a patient explanation, a pat on the back… all of these are small reflections that speak of the totality of Love.

Because of a limitation in perspective, we tend to believe that the sky and the clouds are on the same level and are therefore equivalent. A cloudy sky seems to cancel out the blue sky. But this is not so. The sky is infinite and eternal; it is the medium in which our entire world floats. Clouds are local and ephemeral formations, without the slightest ability to alter the nature of the sky in any way.

The same is true of emotions (fear, guilt, anger, shame, etc.) and the expressions of Light (love, peace, joy, fullness, abundance, gratitude, forgiveness, etc.).

Every time we experience an emotion (in ourselves or coming from someone we interact with), it is nothing more than a local and passing phenomenon.

Every time we experience an expression of Light, it means that the clouds have parted and the totality of the sky is shining in our mind.

Every expression of love we receive is therefore a small reflection of God’s infinite Love for us.

Put without religious terminology: if love is the awareness of unity, every gesture inspired by that love is expressing that, in truth, we are one. I love you as myself because you are me.

That Love is always trying to reach us, just as the light of the sun is always “trying” to reach the planet Earth.

Now, the amount of love we receive is the amount of love we are able to accept. No one but ourselves has power over the clouds that cover our sky.

The way to receive more love is by accepting it.

The way to accept it is by giving it.

A practice:

Infinite Love, the source of your existence, wants to reach the people around you through your smiling “good morning,” through your message to a friend, through your hug to someone who needs it. Every expression of love has a healing effect, both for the one who receives and for the one who gives.

I invite you today to offer yourself as a channel for that Love. Observe your mind, attentive to any loving impulse that wants to pass through you, and dare to let it be expressed. The gesture may seem small in appearance, but it will carry within it the essence of the Totality, which never ceases to say:

“I love you infinitely, just as you are, now and always. There is nothing to forgive. You are Home.”

With all my love,

A.
