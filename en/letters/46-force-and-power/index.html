Nº 46

Force and Power

Swimming against the current or flowing with it

There are two ways of operating in the world: through force or from power.

Force always generates an equal and opposite force. Newton explained this in his third law, and it applies on all levels.

Power, however, has no opposition.

Force argues why it is right.

Power speaks the truth.

Force demands peace.

Power embodies peace.

Force achieves things through effort, swimming against the current.

Power achieves things by allowing them to happen, flowing with the current.

Force struggles to find the next verse.

Power opens its hands and lets it land.

Force seeks to control and fight.

Power observes and allows.

Force punishes itself after every little fall.

Power trusts the process.

Force tries to lift the myriad boats one by one.

Power raises the level of the sea.

Force seeks to dominate nature, in the sense of subjugating it.

Power seeks to dominate it, in the sense of knowing it deeply.

When something cannot be solved, force says, “More force is needed!”

Power says, “Let go of resistance. Forgive the resentment. The solution, hidden behind it, will reveal itself.”

When you feel overwhelmed by the number of things you have to do, force says, “Do more, and do it faster!”

Power says: "Stop. Breathe. Go for a walk and ask a tree how it organizes itself. It will tell you it doesn’t know what 'organizing' is: everything is already in order. It will tell you that it does nothing: it simply is what it is. The fruits come when it is time to bear fruit, and the leaves fall when it is time to let them go. But it does nothing, and if it does, it does not do it alone."

With all my love,

A.
