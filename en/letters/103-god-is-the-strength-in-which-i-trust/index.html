God Is the Strength in Which I Trust

What is in my heart that longs to come into the world?

Since my arrival in Madrid, my mind has been very active, trying to find a way to support myself financially in a way that was fully aligned with my spiritual vocation. Very beautiful things have happened, some truly miraculous, suggesting that I was on the right path. Yet I felt that something was missing, as if the branches were striving to bear flowers and fruit without a central trunk to sustain and nourish them.

In these past few days, the Spirit has been guiding me, more and more clearly, toward the element that was missing from the equation: music.

If I were not afraid; if I could forget about money, knowing that I will always be fully sustained by God’s love, what would I do? What is in my heart that longs to come into the world, not to gain anything external, but out of pure enthusiasm, love, and gratitude to God?

Tonight I was meditating on today’s lesson from A Course in Miracles: “God is the strength in which I trust.” As soon as I began, I finally got it. I truly felt the peace of knowing that God takes care of everything. I only have to be honest with myself and bring into the world what is in my heart.

Then I had a vision of the presentation of my album. I saw that fear had led me to dissociate, almost “forgetting” that I was holding a wonderful album in my hands, one that deserves to see the light. As I recognized the truth, I saw all the fears trying to hide it again: that no one would come to the concert, that the pressure to attract people would disconnect me from trust and make me work from the wrong place, that the whole process would be too expensive… and above all, that I would have to face my own insecurities as a musician, with my voice, with my technique... But all those fears, empty illusions, vanished with the arrival of the simple truth: “God is the strength in which I trust.”

What I saw then was that nothing external mattered at all. If everything turns out to be a failure, so be it, it would simply be another opportunity to let go. But no, what I saw was that the only real reason to give that concert was to glorify God. To lift my music as an offering to Him. To lift myself as an offering, in gratitude for these gifts, these beautiful songs, and all the miracles that have made it possible for me to still sing, and for my music to come into the world.

I did not see all this in my mind, as thoughts, but in my heart, like a flame of devotion. As I connected with that light, all fear dissolved, and in its place there remained only a love overflowing in my chest. At the center of my inflamed heart was the answer to my constant prayers over these past months: Lord, what do You want me to do? Which path leads me to my fullness?

To release the album, present it, and share the entire process, inviting all who wish to walk alongside me to take part in it.

The answer, as usual, has been a finger pointing toward what frightened me most. But if the answer had stopped there, it would have been incomplete. One knows it is the Holy Spirit speaking when all those fears, once as tall as dragons, dissolve, and in their place remains a tender love and the certainty that it will not be I who walks the path, but God through me.

So be it!

With all my love,

A.
