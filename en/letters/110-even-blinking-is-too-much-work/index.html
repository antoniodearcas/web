110. Even blinking is too much work

There is an infinite strength that lifts us and guides us.

This week marks one month since I opened a meditation group called Between Heaven and Earth.

It has been a beautiful path.

Every morning, Monday through Friday, I have been guiding the same meditation, iterating, going a little deeper each day, letting go of the identities that would say, “who are you to teach this?”

The answer to that question has been arriving, again and again, at the climax of each meditation. And it does not speak only about me, but about all of us.

Today, I share it here as well.

Today I woke up tired. Thoughts about all the things I want to do began to pass through my mind, and almost immediately I felt overwhelmed, wanting to escape into my phone or go back to sleep. I felt that I did not have the strength to build the life I want. But that apparent weakness is not the problem; it is only a symptom of the oldest problem of all: the illusion of being separate, of believing that everything depends on ourselves and our limited strength.

There is another way.

An hour later, while guiding a meditation for my community, this idea returned to me:

For a sage, even blinking is too much work.

Years ago I heard this idea, and I did not understand it. Today I looked at it again, but this time under a much wider light, and this is what it showed me:

In reality, there is only one will, and it is the Will of God. There is only one intelligence guiding everything that happens, and that Intelligence has no divisions. Every time you blink, it is not you who does it, it is the entire Universe. There is no separation between your body and everything else. The whole Universe is required for you to take your next breath.

The human mind, before being trained, lives compressed within a body, believing itself to be isolated from everything else. From there, it thinks it is responsible for everything, and exhausts itself trying to control it all.

But within us lies the potential to blossom, to open the mind like a lotus flower with infinite petals. As this happens, our perspective expands radically. We begin to see that our mind is not separate from everything else. The deeper patterns that govern the world begin to reveal themselves. Our prayers begin to receive tangible answers. And mornings no longer feel the same, because we know that behind our apparent weakness, there is an infinite strength that lifts us and guides us.

In this way, we learn to lean more and more into that strength until, finally, even blinking is too much work.

With all my love,

Antonio
