Antonio de Arcas

Musician, writer and contemplative

“You have been lost, but now you are back home. You are infinitely loved, just as you are right now. There is nothing to forgive.”

Antonio de Arcas is a musician, writer and spiritual guide from Madrid. In 2022 he moved to Boston to study at Berklee College of Music, but soon after arriving he met a spiritual teacher in whom he recognized his true reason for being there. Three years later he returned to Spain with the treasures of that period of deep transformation: inspired songs, miraculous stories and embodied teachings.

His life is now devoted to one mission: restoring the sacred in himself and in his community through creativity. And what is the sacred? The sacred is you: your true identity, prior to the illusion of separation; the part of you that knows itself as eternally innocent, loved and free.

A golden seed

Almost ten years ago, when I was 21, I was in a dark place. After years of crisis, I tried to use my intellect to solve my suffering, and it led me straight into solipsism and absurdism.

One day, in despair, I called on the God of my childhood. To my infinite surprise, He picked up the phone. He didn't answer with words, but with a presence that was no different from my own, yet embraced everything. For four hours I was immersed in that ocean of love, until the veil fell over the world again. I didn't understand what had just happened, but I knew my life would never be the same. Soon after, I left my budding career as an engineer and jumped into the void without a plan B.

That experience was like receiving a golden seed. It stayed buried deep in my mind, dormant, waiting for the right conditions to sprout. It finally did during the pandemic. Since then I have watered it every day through contemplation, meditation and the study of different spiritual traditions: Taoism, Zen, Advaita Vedanta, Christian mysticism and A Course in Miracles.

A voice that healed

After four years unable to sing because of recurring pharyngitis, my voice was finally healed at the hands of my teacher. A month later, as a fruit of that healing, I recorded Silent Transmission with my Berklee friends: an album that captures the luminous, silent energy of spiritual liberation. Each song is an answer to a prayer: What is my true path? Who will I be if I let go of everything I think I am? Is death real?

What I share

Every Sunday I write Esferas, a letter from the gap in our soul that seems to separate us from the love of God. I care for a contemplative community, Entre el Cielo y la Tierra, and offer one-to-one spiritual accompaniment to those who have been searching for a long time without quite arriving home. And I also give concerts, like the one I describe in this letter.

In short, my life is guided by the words of Jesus: “Seek first the Kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added unto you.” If this resonates with your path, let us walk together.
